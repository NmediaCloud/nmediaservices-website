The opportunity

Most teams don't need a new image. They need the right one, today.

Businesses are turning to AI generators for everyday visuals: a menu photo, a product backdrop, a science explainer shot. Each request is billed per attempt, takes several tries to get right, and runs a GPU every single time, even when the result looks like something that already exists.

Stockflow flips that. Every asset is produced once, checked by a working creative director, and then licensed again and again. The buyer gets a finished, high-resolution file with clear rights, at a lower price, in the time it takes to search.

Keeping it green

Every download is a generation that never has to run.

AI image and video generation is energy hungry, and the cost repeats for every person who asks for a similar picture. A 2023 study of AI models measured image generation at an average of about 2.9 kWh for every 1,000 images, among the most energy-intensive AI tasks tested. Video costs far more.

A curated library spreads the energy of making an asset across everyone who uses it. Serving a file that already exists costs a small fraction of generating a new one, so the more the library is reused, the lower the footprint of each use.

Source: Luccioni, Jernite and Strubell, "Power Hungry Processing: Watts Driving the Cost of AI Deployment?", 2023.
