Most trading projects are published when they find something. This one is published because it did not. The panel above is the running application, not a mockup: nineteen hypotheses tested, nineteen dead, nothing blocked and nothing live. A research platform that reaches a confident no is doing its job.

01The question

Every strategy idea reduces to one question: does this have an edge, after costs, on data it has never seen? The platform exists to answer that in about an hour and to make the answer defensible. It is built on NautilusTrader, with walk-forward analysis, cross-instrument transfer testing, and a set of guards whose only job is to stop a backtest from flattering itself.

03The candidate that looked real

The most promising result was mean reversion on H1, with 78% fold consistency. It turned out to be a directional bet wearing a disguise. The optimiser had pinned the RSI thresholds to the edges of their range, producing a rule that almost never buys and sells constantly. That matched the training window and failed on every other instrument, and on the same pair in a later period.

"Finding that out cost an afternoon. Finding it out live would have cost the account."

04The one that was supposed to survive

For a while the non-farm payrolls reaction was the live candidate. At the release minute the move was many times the cost of trading it, the best economics measured anywhere in the project, and the only thing missing was consensus forecast data to call direction. That data was quoted at $50 to $150 a month.

It turned out to be obtainable free. With it, the hypothesis was tested properly across 217 events and died like the rest: r = +0.08, t = +1.18, and 51.4% of signs correct. A coin flip with a spread attached. The register now shows that blocker resolved and the hypothesis dead, which is the honest end to the most exciting idea in the project.

07What is built, and what is deliberately not

The panel tracks build phases as well as hypotheses, and half of them are gated on purpose.

So the risk engine, the broker adapter and the ML layer are specified and not built. That is not a hole in the project. It is the project declining to build the expensive parts for a strategy that does not work yet.

08Why publish a negative result

A research platform that only reports wins is a marketing device. This one reports what it found, which was nothing, across six asset classes and nineteen ideas. The infrastructure is the deliverable: the walk-forward harness, the transfer test, the cost accounting and the guards that caught eight real bugs. Those carry to the next question. The strategies never did.

Want research infrastructure that argues back?

The value here is not the strategies. It is the guards that stop a backtest from lying to you.
